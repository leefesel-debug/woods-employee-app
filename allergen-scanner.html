Photograph the ingredients, check the extracted allergens, and submit for approval. Existing products can be rescanned and compared.

Food safety: Scanning is an aid, not an allergen verification. Check the original packaging, supplier details, and kitchen cross-contact risks. Pilot scans and approvals do not change the live matrix. Production updates will only be enabled after testing and separate sign-off. “May contain” is kept separate from ingredients.
